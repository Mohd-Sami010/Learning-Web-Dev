Unity Projects

Neural Prison

A 3D platformer built in Unity where the player controls a cube that is constantly pushed forward and can only move left or right. Instead of traditional death, obstacles push the player back, creating a unique gameplay loop. The game features 20 handcrafted levels, glitch effects, and an AI-driven narrative told through robotic voiceovers and hidden human messages.

- This was my first game that I really finished and published it online.

What I Learned:
- Unity Events, Coroutines, lerp and other C# functions.
- UI design.
- Designing unique core mechanics (non-lethal obstacles that push the player back).
- Creating narrative through environment and voiceovers without traditional cutscenes.
- Implementing glitch effects, fake shutdowns, and mystery elements to enhance atmosphere.
- Balancing difficulty progression across 20 handcrafted levels.
- Managing a full project solo: coding, level design, and publishing.

My Role: Solo developer (design, coding, narrative, level creation).
